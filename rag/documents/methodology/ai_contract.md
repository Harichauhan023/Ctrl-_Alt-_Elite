# Explainable-AI Contract

## Explainable-AI contract
The AI layer explains but never computes. It receives only structured facts produced by the deterministic engine plus retrieved knowledge chunks, and must not invent geographic facts or alter any numeric score. When every LLM provider is unavailable, a deterministic rule-based explainer produces the same JSON shape so the product keeps working.
