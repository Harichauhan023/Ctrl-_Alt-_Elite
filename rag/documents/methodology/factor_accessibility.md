# Accessibility Factor Deep-Dive

## Accessibility factor deep-dive
Accessibility blends 60% proximity to MAJOR roads (OSM motorway/trunk/primary/secondary; 1.2 km exponential decay) with 40% road density within 600 m (km of road per km², saturated at 9). Sites more than 5 km from ANY road are capped at 20 as a soft penalty — the planning analogy of a reachability threshold.
