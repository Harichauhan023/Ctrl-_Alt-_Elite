# Land-Use Factor Deep-Dive

## Land-use factor deep-dive
Each business type owns a category→suitability matrix. EV favours commercial (95) and mixed-use (85); warehouses want industrial (100) and reject residential (15); telecom accepts agricultural land (85); renewables strongly prefer agricultural (90). 'unknown' defaults to a neutral 50. Overlaps resolve with protected zones winning first.
