# Hard Constraints vs Soft Penalties

## Hard constraints vs soft penalties
Some conditions invalidate a site instead of lowering its score. A site on protected land (lake, river corridor) or inside a critical flood-risk pocket is marked NOT_SUITABLE regardless of its numeric score. Lower-impact conditions apply a soft penalty only. The API always returns applied constraints separately from factor scores.
