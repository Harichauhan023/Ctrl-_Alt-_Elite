# FAQ — Weights & Calibration

## FAQ: how to choose weights
Start from the business-type presets, then adjust to the decision-maker's priorities. Weights must total 100%; the frontend shows the live sum and the backend renormalises within 5% and rejects anything further off with a 400 error. Useful sensitivity test: swap population and accessibility weights and watch which zones keep their rank.
