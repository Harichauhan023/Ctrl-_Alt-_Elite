# FAQ — Scores & Status Bands

## FAQ: what the readiness score means
The 0-100 score is a weighted index, not a probability. 80+ is labelled High potential, 65-79 Good, 50-64 Moderate, 35-49 Weak, below 35 Low. It compares locations against each other within this study area; it is not an absolute real-world standard for land value or revenue.

## FAQ: what NOT_SUITABLE means
NOT_SUITABLE means a hard constraint fired: the point sits on protected land (lake or river corridor) or inside a critical flood pocket. The numeric factor scores remain visible for transparency — demand may exist — but the platform refuses to recommend the site. This mirrors planning rules where no score overrides a legal or safety constraint.
