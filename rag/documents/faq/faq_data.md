# FAQ — Data & Synthesis

## FAQ: why synthetic data
Hackathon pragmatism with engineering honesty: perfect municipal datasets take weeks to license. The architecture makes every layer replaceable — the synthetic population, land-use and risk layers are seeded from real Rajkot geography and clearly labelled, so a government GeoAPI or census file drops straight in.
