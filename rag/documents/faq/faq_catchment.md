# FAQ — Catchment Accuracy

## FAQ: how accurate are catchment numbers
They are planning-grade estimates, not traffic simulations. Straight-line rings at 18 km/h ignore one-way streets, congestion and signal delays; in dense cores real 10-minute reach can be 20-40% smaller. Use them to compare sites relative to each other, which is robust to the simplification.
