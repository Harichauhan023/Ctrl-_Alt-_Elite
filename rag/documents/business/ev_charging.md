# EV Charging Station Business Guide

## EV charging station siting rules
EV charging stations benefit above all from excellent road accessibility and visible demand: drivers favour chargers on major roads, commercial corridors and destinations. Typical weights: accessibility 35%, population 20%, competition 20%, environment 15%, land use 10%. A small competitor presence can validate an emerging corridor, but a tight cluster saturates it. Flood-prone plots threaten electrical assets and uptime.

## EV charging expansion playbook
A practical EV rollout: shortlist zones with accessibility above 70 and population above 60 on the heatmap, eliminate anything within 1 km of two or more rivals, then walk the top cells for grid access and land tenure. Ring roads and highway junctions (e.g. Greenland Chowkdi) score on access; dense cores score on demand. Balance both anchors.
