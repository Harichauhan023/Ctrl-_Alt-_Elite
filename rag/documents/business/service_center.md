# Service Center Business Guide

## Service center siting rules
Service centers need reachability by the largest customer base: population 30% and accessibility 30% dominate. Commercial or mixed-use land near arterial roads is preferred; competition is moderate (15%) because overlapping service networks still share demand. The 20-minute catchment is the realistic service promise.
