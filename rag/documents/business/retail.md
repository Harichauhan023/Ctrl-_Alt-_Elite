# Retail Store Business Guide

## Retail store siting rules
Retail is population- and footfall-driven: population 30%, accessibility 25%, competition 20%, land use 15%, environment 10%. Unlike most businesses, retail can prefer competitor presence because commercial clusters signal established customer traffic (agglomeration); in 'attract' mode more nearby rivals RAISE the competition score. Commercial zoning is ideal.

## Retail catchment playbook
For retail, read the 10-minute catchment as the primary trading area and 20-minute as secondary. A 10-minute shed above ~150k people in this modelled dataset indicates a strong urban catchment. Compare that against competitor counts inside the same shed before committing lease capital.
