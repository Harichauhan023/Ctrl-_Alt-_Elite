# Renewable Energy Facility Business Guide

## Renewable energy facility siting rules
Small renewable facilities prioritise open land and low environmental conflict: environment 40%, land use 30%, accessibility 20%. Agricultural (90) or industrial (80) plots suit solar deployments; protected land is prohibited; population proximity is largely irrelevant except for evacuation and safety buffers.
