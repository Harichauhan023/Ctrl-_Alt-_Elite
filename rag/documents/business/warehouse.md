# Warehouse & Fulfilment Business Guide

## Warehouse siting rules
Warehouses need highway-grade accessibility and suitable zoning far more than nearby population: land use 40%, accessibility 35%, environment 15%, population 5%, competition 5%. Industrial land is ideal; residential is poor. Flood risk matters because stored inventory is immobile — a medium-risk band is a planning flag, high is close to a veto.

## Warehouse logistics playbook
For logistics, hunt the heatmap edges: high accessibility with industrial/brownfield land and low population scores are features, not bugs. Highway junctions on the ring road beat the dense core. Always export the report's nearest-major-road metric — under 0.5 km is highway-grade in this dataset.
