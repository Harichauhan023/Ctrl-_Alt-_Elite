# Telecom Tower Business Guide

## Telecom tower siting rules
Telecom towers maximise population coverage per site, so population (25%) and environment (20%) carry weight. Agricultural (85) and industrial (80) land are often fine; protected is prohibited. Existing towers indicate coverage overlap rather than demand saturation, so competition weight stays low (10%). Elevated flood risk threatens ground equipment.
