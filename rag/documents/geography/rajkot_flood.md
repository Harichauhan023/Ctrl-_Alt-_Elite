# Aji River Flood Risk

## Aji river flood-risk model
The Aji river crosses southern Rajkot west-to-east near latitude 22.28-22.30. The synthetic risk layer buffers it into medium (~850 m), high (~350 m) bands plus one critical pocket at a river bend near the old city (about 22.283, 70.793). Real deployments would replace these with municipal flood maps via the same pipeline.
