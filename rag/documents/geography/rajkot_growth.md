# Rajkot Growth Directions

## Rajkot growth directions
The population model fades northward beyond the ring road: Greenland Chowkdi in the north anchors a highway junction with strong accessibility but thin current population — a classic bet on future growth. Western corridors (Kalawad Road, airport direction) are the established expansion axis.
