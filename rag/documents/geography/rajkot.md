# Rajkot Study Area

## Rajkot study area overview
Rajkot (Saurashtra, Gujarat) is a manufacturing and trading hub of roughly 1.5-2 million people. The modelled study area spans the urban core inside an ~18x23 km window: a dense old city, radial arterial roads, the 150 Feet Ring Road, southward industrial growth along Gondal Road and the Aji river crossing the southern city west-to-east.

## Rajkot commercial corridors
In the modelled layers, commercial intensity concentrates in the old city core (around 22.298, 70.794), the Kalawad Road corridor west, the 150 Feet Ring Road western strip, and the Mavdi Chowk junction south-west. Gondal Road runs as a south-eastern mixed-use growth corridor. These are indicative zones for analysis, not legal zoning maps.
