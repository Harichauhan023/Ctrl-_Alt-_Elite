# Playbook — Market Entry

## Playbook: entering a new market area
For market entry, start with the heatmap's High band, then interview the top zones: check 1 km competitor counts below 3, 10-minute catchment above the second-quartile of your current sites, and no risk above medium. Export each shortlisted report before field visits; the JSON anchors every claim.
