# Playbook — Climate-Resilient Siting

## Playbook: climate-resilient siting
For resilience-first siting, raise environment weight to 30-40% and treat any risk above medium as disqualifying. The Aji flood bands show exactly where scores collapse despite strong demand — a recurring pattern in Indian monsoon cities. Critical pockets are always vetoed by hard constraint regardless of weight.
