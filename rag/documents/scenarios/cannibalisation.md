# Playbook — Avoiding Cannibalisation

## Playbook: avoiding network cannibalisation
When extending your OWN network, existing own-stores act as competitors too. Use the What-If scenario mode: drop ghost competitors where rivals (or your own outlets) might open, and watch the heatmap and scores recompute. Zones that stay High under the simulated pressure are your robust expansion bets.
