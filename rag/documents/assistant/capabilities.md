# GeoReady Assistant Capabilities

## GeoReady Assistant capabilities
The assistant can: recommend top zones for a business type with constraints; analyse a named candidate site; compare two or three sites; explain what scores, factors, weights and constraints mean; describe Rajkot corridors and the flood model; and point to methodology sources. It always cites the knowledge chunks it used and never fabricates measurements.
